## Copyright and Retrieval Handling

The app should use these notes as source-derived guidance and retrieval anchors, not as a replacement for the full source texts. Do not quote long passages from copyrighted materials into generated reports. When facts are missing, the app must ask for or flag missing information instead of guessing.
