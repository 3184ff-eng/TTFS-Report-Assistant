# Frequente Industrial Park Fire Investigation Report Structure

Source: `Building 10 Frequente Ind. Park 12.02.18.docx`, supplied by the user as an example of a full fire investigation report structure.

Use this source only as a structure and style reference. Do not reuse incident facts, names, addresses, findings, values, or conclusions from this example in any other report.

## Example Report Structure

The example report uses this broad structure:

1. Cover/title information
   - Report title
   - Incident location
   - Date of fire
   - Date of investigation
   - Investigation team and roles

2. Table of Contents
   - Definitions
   - Overview
   - General Information
   - The Investigation
   - Arrival
   - Mapping the Scene
   - Scene Examination
   - External Examination
   - Internal Examination
   - Origin
   - Sector of Origin
   - Area of Origin
   - Point of Origin
   - Ignition Sources
   - Samples Taken
   - Conclusion
   - Appendices

3. Definitions
   - Technical terms used in the report
   - Electrical/fire pattern terms such as arcing, arc mapping, area of origin, beading, fire patterns, point of origin, radius of error, sleeving, smoldering, soot, and spalling

4. Overview
   - Call receipt and incident summary
   - Ownership/occupancy summary
   - Firefighting operations summary
   - Reason investigation was requested
   - Investigation dates and team
   - High-level conclusion summary

5. General Information
   - Date/time of fire
   - Time of call
   - Address
   - First officer at scene
   - Station ground/responding appliances
   - Description of premises
   - Building construction
   - Occupants/businesses
   - Fire protection systems
   - Electrical service/distribution where relevant
   - Situations and actions before Fire Service arrival
   - Approximate values/losses

6. Investigation
   - Arrival
   - Mapping the scene
   - Scene examination
   - External examination
   - Internal examination
   - Sector/area/point of origin analysis
   - Ignition source analysis
   - Samples taken

7. Conclusion
   - Area of origin
   - Point of origin where supported
   - Most probable ignition source
   - First material ignited
   - Fire spread/development explanation
   - Cause classification

8. Appendices
   - Photographic illustrations
   - Drawings and site plan illustrations
   - Pre-investigation risk assessment
   - Collection and packaging of evidence
   - Related reports
   - Statements

## App Guidance

For a full investigation report generator, use this structure as a template for a comprehensive narrative report. For the official TTFS Fire Report Form, map only the relevant condensed content into official form fields and put overflow or extended analysis into appendices.

Keep these separations clear:

- Direct Fire Service observations: Officer's Observations
- Witness statements and pre-arrival facts: Additional Information or witness section
- Origin/cause analysis: investigation analysis or Additional Information, not direct observations unless actually observed by the reporting officer
- Evidence handling: evidence log/appendix
- Photos and diagrams: photographic and drawing appendices
