## Chemical and Flammable Material Prompts

When chemicals may be involved, prompt for:

- Chemical identity or trade name
- Safety data sheet availability
- Quantity and storage arrangement
- Physical state: solid, liquid, gas, vapor, aerosol, dust, cryogen
- Flash point, vapor pressure, boiling point, vapor density, flammable limits, and ignition temperature where relevant
- Oxidizing, water-reactive, toxic, corrosive, or explosive properties
- Incompatible materials
- Ventilation and containment
- Spill, leak, or release pathway
- Firefighting agent used and compatibility

## Scene Safety

Flag chemical scenes for safety review where information is missing. Consider:

- Toxic exposure
- Corrosive exposure
- Oxygen displacement
- Flammable vapor accumulation
- Reactive mixtures
- Pressure vessel or compressed gas hazards
- Cryogenic hazards
- Contaminated runoff

Do not advise physical handling of hazardous chemicals without appropriate PPE, authority, and specialist review.
